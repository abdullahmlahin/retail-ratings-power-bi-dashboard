# Retail Transaction Ratings Dashboard

A Power BI group project analyzing customer ratings of retail transactions.

**Business problem:** Where is customer satisfaction strong or weak across brands, cities and age groups?

**What it includes**
- Average rating KPI cards
- Ratings and orders by age group against a 4.0 target
- Rating mix by city group
- Product category by city matrix
- Brand/item scatter of rating vs. sales that surfaces the lowest-rated brand and city
- Slicers for item, brand, age group and city group

**Skills shown:** KPI design, conditional measures, interactive slicers, storytelling with data, teamwork.

Built for Bentley University's *Data Analytics for Accountants* course (AC 770).

*Data files and course answer keys are not included in this repository.*
